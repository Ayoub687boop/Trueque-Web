Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Trueque is a platform for discovering and exchanging items with other users. This policy explains the basic way we handle information.

User content

Product listings and profile information may be visible to other users depending on how the app is used.

Data retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the service, safety, legal, or operational purposes.

Contact
