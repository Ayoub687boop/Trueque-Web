Community Guidelines

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Be honest

Describe your items clearly, use real photos, and do not hide important defects.

Be respectful

Treat other users with respect. Harassment, threats, hate, or abusive behavior are not allowed.

Trade safely

Meet in safe public places when exchanging locally. Do not share unnecessary personal information.

Do not post prohibited items

Illegal, stolen, dangerous, counterfeit, or harmful items are not allowed.

Report problems

If something feels wrong, report it so we can review it.
